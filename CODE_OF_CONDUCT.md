# Code of Conduct

ECHO//RUN welcomes contributors and players of different backgrounds and
experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Give credit for work and disclose relevant third-party sources.
- Help keep issue and review discussions focused on actionable outcomes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, impersonation, and knowingly false attribution are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary. For sensitive conduct
concerns, contact the primary maintainer through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/eiei-ee). Security issues must
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
