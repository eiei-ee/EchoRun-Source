# Echo Megacity Districts

Two original trackside city modules authored locally in Blender for EchoRun.

- Source: `ArtSource/Blender/EchoMegacityDistricts.blend`
- Generator: `Tools/Art/create_echo_megacity_districts.py`
- Production models: `Models/EchoMegacityDistrictA.fbx` and `Models/EchoMegacityDistrictB.fbx`
- Intended bounds per module: about 15 m long, 6 m deep, 11.6 m high
- Pivot: bottom center
- Materials: five persistent EchoRun palette materials
- Gameplay role: decorative trackside city; no colliders
- Provenance: created locally for this repository; no third-party geometry or textures

The paired variants use different tower rhythms, facade window grids, signage,
utility spines, roof equipment, and foreground braces. Unity remaps their named
materials to the persistent EchoRun palette and places them outside the playable
lane and connector envelope.
